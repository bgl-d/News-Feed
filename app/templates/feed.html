The Nobel Prize in Physics in 2026

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics was awarded to Francis Halzen for his foundational role in establishing the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole and detecting high-energy cosmic neutrinos. His pioneering work opened a new era of multi-messenger astronomy by capturing non-light particles from distant cosmic phenomena.
